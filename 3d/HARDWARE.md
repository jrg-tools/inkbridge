## Bambu A1 Starting Profile

- **Filament type:** Generic PLA
- **Nozzle:** 205°C
- **Bed:** 55–60°C
- **Part cooling:** 100%
- **Print speed:** 30–70 mm/s for conservative printing
- **Drying:** 55°C for ~6 h if moisture is suspected

## Notes

- The **±0.02 mm** specification refers to filament diameter tolerance, not printed-part dimensional accuracy.
- Compatible with standard **1.75 mm FDM/FFF 3D printers**.
- Suitable for general-purpose PLA printing.
